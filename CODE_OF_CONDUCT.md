# Code of conduct

tpt-dynamics is maintained by TPT Solutions. We want participation in issues,
discussions and any future pull requests to be respectful, constructive and
welcoming to everyone, whatever their background or experience.

## Expected behaviour

* Be respectful and considerate in written communication.
* Assume good intent, and ask before assuming bad intent.
* Give and accept technical criticism on the work, not on the person.
* Credit the work of others, and say when you are unsure.
* Keep discussion on the project's scope: physics correctness, data formats,
  performance and licensing.

## Unacceptable behaviour

* Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, or sexualised content.
* Publishing someone's private information without their consent.
* Sustained disruption of issues or discussions.
* Submitting material you do not have the right to contribute under the
  project's licences (see `CONTRIBUTING.md`).

## Reporting

Report concerns privately through GitHub's private reporting for this repository,
or to the maintainers at TPT Solutions by the contact route listed on the
repository. Reports are reviewed by the maintainers, who will respond within a
reasonable time and keep the reporter's identity confidential where possible.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may remove, edit or reject comments, issues and contributions that
break this code, and may restrict or block participants who repeatedly do so.
Decisions are explained to the participant concerned.

## Attribution

This summary follows the spirit of the Contributor Covenant, version 2.1
(<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>), which
