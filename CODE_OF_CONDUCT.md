# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We want participation in tpt-dynamics to be a harassment-free experience for
everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability,
ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of
experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal
appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being respectful and constructive in issues, pull requests and discussions.
- Giving and accepting technical feedback gracefully.
- Acknowledging when you were wrong and crediting others' work.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, or trolling.
- Publishing others' private information without permission.
- Sexualised content or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Any other conduct that could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This code applies in project spaces (the repository, its issues, pull requests,
discussions, and documentation) and when an individual represents the project
in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Report unacceptable behaviour privately through GitHub's reporting tools for
this repository, or to the maintainers at TPT Solutions. Reports are reviewed
and handled confidentially. Maintainers may take any action they consider
appropriate, from a private warning to a permanent ban from project spaces.
